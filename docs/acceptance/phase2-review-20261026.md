## Finding fixed: camera churn beyond the retention radius

On main the Market is kept loaded while the camera frustum intersects its envelope, and released the instant it leaves the frustum once the player is beyond the 110 m retention radius. A reproduction (`preview/tests/commons-stream.test.js`, orbit regression) with four slow 3.6 s camera orbits rebuilt the destination five times at 150 m and nine times at 250 m. Each rebuild constructs about 3.05 MB of geometry (20–25 ms on the main thread in Node, before tree instancing, canvas textures and GPU uploads in the browser) and then disposes it. The corrected adapter keeps a destination required for 15 s after it last left the frustum, so the orbits build once; sustained looking away beyond the radius still releases it. Two further corrections: the scheduler uses a chunk's measured size instead of its authored estimate once it has loaded (the Market measures 8.24 MB resident in the browser against a 3 MiB estimate), and the loading/stall/retry notices name "Bayou Market Commons" as the HUD does.

## Finding fixed: session expiry re-spawned a reconnecting player at the District spawn

Every merged-head journey saw both clients' town sockets close with code 4001 about 60 minutes in (the fixture identity's expiry), and from run 3 the receipts record the close reasons. The server has two expiry checks: the broadcast loop closes the socket with "Session expired. Reconnecting securely." and the close handler keeps the player in the world for the 30 s departure grace, so the page's immediate secure reconnect resumes in place; but the per-command check (the next pose, 200 ms later at most) went through `disconnectUser`, which leaves the world at once, so the reconnect re-joined as a new arrival at the District walk spawn. A player walking the Market plaza at that moment was moved about 180 m back to the District spawn with no notice beyond the one-second reconnect gate. In the journeys this showed as player 0 "wedged" on the lap-22 return in runs 4 and 6 (teleported to the spawn, then walked straight into the glass building east of it, where the navigation has no route) and as the lap-22 detour in run 5; the pre-merge attempt 2 stall at lap 22 was the same event.

Fix (`server/app.js`): an expired session on the command path now closes the socket exactly as the broadcast loop does, keeping the departure grace; a ban or ended approval still disconnects and leaves immediately. Regression (`server/tests/app.test.js`): for each path the identity is expired right after a broadcast, the socket must close with 4001 "Session expired. Reconnecting securely.", the player must still be in the world, and a reconnect must resume the same player at the position from before the expiry (one join, not two). The command-path case fails on the previous server with "Please sign in again." and passes with the fix; the server suites around sockets, departures and account deletion pass (27 passed, 2 Redis skips).

## Finding fixed: the hourly sign-in expiry was a visible gate (takeover, 2026-10-28)

Where the hour comes from: `server/tests/browser-fixture.js` stamps every fixture identity `expiresAt: Date.now() + 3,600,000` on each authenticate, and the town keeps the identity a socket was issued at upgrade (`server/app.js`), so each socket's identity runs out an hour after it opened, whatever the sign-in's own life (the dev-auth and WorkOS session records last seven days). With a provider sign-in the same identity carries the access token's `exp` capped by that record (`server/auth.js`, `server/redis-auth.js`). The hosted gateway retires every socket at 270 s with a rotate notice, so there the page moves to a fresh socket before any token expiry; the standalone server (local, desktop, the fixture) had no such move, so the first sign the page got was the 4001 close itself: `lost()` cleared the snapshot and the remote avatars, showed the gate with "Connection lost. Rejoining the town…", and re-admitted the page about a second later. Every player, every hour, mid-walk.

Fix (`server/app.js`): each socket schedules the existing rotate notice (`server/connection-close.js`) for 15 s before its identity expires, or for `connectionLifetime` when that is sooner; the page opens a fresh socket with a fresh ticket, which re-authenticates and gets a fresh expiry, switches over on its first snapshot and never shows the gate, exactly as for the hosted request limit. A page that does not move is still closed at the expiry with 4001 and keeps its place through the departure grace (the previous correction), and the client (`preview/src/connection-policy.js`) now names that close "Refreshing your sign-in…" (kind `refreshing`, not terminal) rather than a lost connection; any other 4001 reason keeps its meaning until the ticket decides. Regressions: `server/tests/app.test.js` (an identity 1.5 s from expiry receives the notice before it, with `closeInMs` and a connection id; a fresh socket presenting `continues` resumes the same player at the moved position with one join; the old socket is retired; on the previous server the case fails by its 10 s timeout because no notice ever arrives) and `preview/tests/connection-policy.test.js`. Suites with both server corrections: 28 passed and 2 Redis skips across the socket, account-deletion, cycling and voice files; 31 passed and 2 skips across the client-notice, streaming and rotation files. The journey harness already accounts planned rotations through the client's own counter, so a rerun on the final head should show two rotations and no 4001 closes; that rerun is queued below. The hosted gateway is unchanged.

Also found on the same read, and already fixed elsewhere: on the Terraces and Woodland revisions the Heights stream dropped District points and an empty update kept every resident Heights chunk in place for as long as the player stayed in the District; #729 (`323aff81`) corrects it with regressions and owns that file.
