# Continuous travel and spatial streaming

Implement #582 and #583 against the Phase 1 manifest and authored Commons footprint. Two players must walk District → Commons → District in the same document, identity, avatar and authoritative session. Preserve camera orientation and movement on successful crossings. Collision and shared simulation remain resident, independent of visual asset availability. Reserved manifest locations remain inaccessible.

Use a deterministic distance-based presentation scheduler with bounded resident and in-flight chunks, prefetch distance, unload hysteresis, bounded retry delay, stale-generation cancellation, and exactly-once disposal. Each owned resource supplies its own cleanup; never dispose District-shared materials through a streamed group. Keep the District presentation resident to preserve its approved composition; expansion presentation is streamed and the scheduler supports further registered chunks. Compare this against eager loading rather than partitioning the server.

Load Commons before arrival, keep it while the local player or camera needs it, and unload after leaving the retention radius. If loading fails, show a polite loading/retry notice and stop only movement into the unavailable footprint; retain camera controls, return movement and the server session. A restored server pose inside the chunk pins its load and prevents further eastward movement until ready while permitting retreat west. Late completion after unload/world replacement must dispose resources without attaching them. Duplicate updates must not duplicate meshes or listeners.

Measure 20 round trips using two real fixture browser clients, mutual authoritative positions, identity/avatar/camera continuity, disconnect/reconnect, failed asset recovery, and no reload. Record geometry/texture/object counts, frame times, heap where available, resource requests and WebSocket bandwidth. Compare District baseline and expanded simulation ticks/snapshot size before proposing partitioning. Tests, screenshots, measurements and local gates are required; hosted/device/human-accessibility acceptance remains separate.

Alternatives considered: eager loading preserves simplicity but cannot bound expansion resources; whole-world replacement resets state and violates continuous travel. A presentation-only scheduler preserves the current authoritative seam and permits incremental expansion.

Implemented limits: the Commons adapter owns one presentation chunk, allows one physical load at a time, caps owned geometry buffers at 1 MiB, prefetches within 85 metres and retains within 110 metres. Camera-frustum intersection also retains visible content. Retry delay starts at one second and backs off to at most 30 seconds. A load still unresolved after 15 seconds stays physically accounted for and reports a stall; the scheduler does not start additional unresolved imports. Once it settles, normal retry can resume. Buffer accounting is exact owned geometry storage, not total GPU memory. The separate browser journey must establish bounded whole-scene resources.
