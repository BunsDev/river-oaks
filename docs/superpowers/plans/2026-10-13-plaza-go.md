## Change

- **`preview/src/go-rules.js`** (new, pure): a 9×9 board.
  - Captures, no suicide, and positional superko (which covers simple ko).
  - Two passes end the game, scored by area (Tromp–Taylor) with 7.5 komi.
  - Points run a–j without i, and games replay exactly.
- **`preview/src/go-opponent.js`** (new): Monte Carlo play-outs that never fill
  their own eyes, spread over candidates by UCB1.
  - One tactical rule: a capture on offer is taken, the biggest first.
  - It passes after yours when ahead, and when nothing is worth playing.
  - Seeded from the position with `Math.imul`, so it's deterministic.
  - With a budget of 700, a move took about 1 s on this loaded machine (900
    play-outs: 1.3 s on average, 2.9 s at worst).
- **`preview/src/table-game-worker.js`:** a `go` entry.
  `plaza-table-games.js` exports `createWorkerThinker`.
- **`preview/src/plaza-salon-plan.js` / `plaza-salon.js`:**
  - `GO` and `goSpot` (points for either chair).
  - A kaya goban with grid and star points, and up to 81 stones, hidden until Go
    is the table's game.
  - The mancala board moved into its own group with `setVisible`, so the table
    swaps boards.
- **`preview/src/plaza-go.js` + `.css`** (new): the goban sheet.
  - Clickable intersections and arrow-key focus. Stones, star points and the
    last move.
  - Captures and the running area count. Pass, and New game.
  - The regular or pass-and-play, black or white.
  - The regular thinks in the worker, with a smaller in-page fallback.
- **Grove switch:** Mancala / Go in both sheets. `main.js` keeps a
  `groveGames()` map and a `switchGrove()` that swaps the boards on the table.
- **Salon Guide:** a Go chapter with territory, capture and ko pictures. The
  capture and ko are checked against the rules (the ko retake is refused).
- **Journeys:** `plaza-go` (new).
