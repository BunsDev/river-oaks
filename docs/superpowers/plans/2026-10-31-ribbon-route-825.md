## Option 1: post spans (Val, 2026-10-31)

Slice 1 gained `maxSpan` (commit `c19a01ba` on #826): a middle post in an exclusion is left out
and the canopy spans over it, with each edge's gap held to `maxSpan`.

Sweep with `maxSpan: 6`, from y -1300 to the lane's north end (-1197.3), widths 2-2.4 m and
offsets 6.5-7 m:

- **Passes only at width 2.0 m, offset 6.8-6.9 m.** The window is 10 cm, so a moved fixture
  can break it.
- Wider walks fail. Below 6.8 m the bin-54 and bench-5 posts both drop out, leaving a 9 m gap.
  At 6.9 m and beyond, the north end post (boundary 34) lands in a door near the lane end.
- Ending just past IPIC (-1222) fails, because 3.12 m modules turn one skipped post into a
  6.2 m gap.

**Committed route:** `config/ribbon/district-spine.json`. It has one segment (`spine`) with 4
points, width 2 m, headroom 3.6 m, `canopy` style and `maxSpan` 6. It starts 7.5 m from the walk
spawn (across the lane, on the east side) and runs past Van Cleef & Arpels and IPIC to the lane's
north end, 102.8 m in all.
- Expansion gives 34 modules, 69 posts, 18 lamps and 34 fans, with 1 post left out
  (`ribbon:spine:post:019:l`, over the Van Cleef & Arpels storefront bench).
- `preview/tests/ribbon-route-district.test.js` rebuilds every exclusion from `district.json`,
  `laneFixtures` and `storefrontBenchSpots` and asserts the result. It also checks that the route
  starts at the spawn, passes IPIC with the walk clear of the kerb and frontage, and is refused
  without spans.
