## Darts design

- **Geometry** (`ipic-plan.js` `IPIC_DARTS`):
  - Board face at d 25.6, centre a 0.1, 1.73 m up.
  - Throwing line at d 23.23 (2.37 m out).
  - Throwing area: a −0.2..1.1, d 22.2..23.23. It clears the east tub chair's server zone, is off every public route, and holds two players.
  - Cabinet collider `ipic-dart-cabinet`: a −0.375..0.575, d 25.55..25.87.
  - The regenerated circulation data is unchanged: no trip width or turning circle moved.
- **Server** (`server/darts-games.js`):
  - **Who plays:** a player at the line is on foot, not sitting, not in an office, in the IPIC room and inside the area.
  - **Commands:** `practice` (against the house `regular`), `invite`/`accept`/`decline`, `throw {aim}` and `resign`.
  - **Landing:** a throw carries only where the meter stopped (|x|, |y| ≤ 230 mm). The server adds 18 mm logistic scatter from `crypto.randomInt` and stores the landing point rounded to 0.1 mm. A message that names its own landing point is refused.
  - **The regular** throws on the server from a seed the server chose (`darts-opponent.js`), as soon as the turn is its own.
  - **Ending:** a checkout, a resignation, or a player leaving the line (walking off, sitting, travelling, disconnecting) ends the match. The line clears once no player is left at it.
  - **Snapshot:** `darts: [{id, players, points, scores, turn, result, invite}]`.
  - **Checkpoint:** optional `dartsGames`, restored with `?? []`, so there is no version bump. This follows the precedent of every table and café game kind. Restore checks the exact keys, the known line id, the points and seed bounds, that the match replays, and that the result agrees with the replay.
- **Client:**
  - `plaza-darts.js` gains `shared`. State is replayed from the snapshot, a throw sends `{aim}`, and Practise / Invite / Accept / Decline / Resign replace the opponent switch.
  - The cabinet board (`ipic-interior.js`, painted by the new shared `dartboard-texture.js`) mirrors every match, panel open or not.
  - `main.js` creates the IPIC instance. "At the line" uses the same rule as the server, and X plays.
  - `window.__riverIpicDarts` is the inspector.

## Axe room design (after the 08:55Z audit of the "room = store" assumptions)

- **Code-only.** A store in `district.json` changes the server world fingerprint (`digest(worldData)`), which forces a production checkpoint migration on deploy. That is held for Val, like the building raise. So the room is planned in code, inside `storeRoomsFor` (`store-rooms.js`), after the approved-interior replacement. It is gated to the River Oaks building id, the way `isIpicStore` is gated.
- **Synthetic `storeId: 'venue:axe-and-darts'`, never null.** A null id counts as outdoors in the same-room checks: talk, sit/stand, water, build, camera and voice. The room uses a stable `index`, and `name` and `summary` are set.
- **No staff resident (`people: []`).** Venue people would become residents, which changes the resident count, so an existing durable checkpoint would fail to restore and the town would not load. `shared-population` must therefore exempt the venue.
- **Placement.** A fixed, pre-validated span on a free street facade, with a test that it sits inside the ring, overlaps no room and is clear of every door and the office lobby. Free spans: see the session survey. The Hopdoddy Burger Bar building's south facade, 1.0–18.3 m along it, is the default proposal.
- **Rendering.**
  - `district.js` must treat the venue as frontage: a glass opening, door, frame and sign. Otherwise the doorway draws as stone party wall.
  - Avoid photographed frontage spans and pier collisions.
  - `store-interiors.js` gets a specialty branch for `axe-darts-v1`. The IPIC dartboard stays `dartboards[0]`.
- **Travel and UI.**
  - `travel {storeId: venueId}` gets a branch to enter and leave the venue.
  - `walking-ui` gets "Step inside" and "Step outside" for the venue.
  - The venue goes in the destination list and Places.
- **Games.**
  - `server/axe-games.js` (6/6 tests) and `axe-rules.js` (4/4) are written.
  - The venue's dartboard reuses `darts-games.js` as a second line, `axe-and-darts-board`.
- **Tests to update.** `storefront-traversal` (rooms equal stores, 30), `ipic-interior` (footprints unchanged), `auto-navigation`, `shared-population` and `store-encounters`. Each should exclude venue rooms explicitly; the assertions stay as strong as they are.
