## Results, gaps and next step

Results: the review found Phase 2's streaming design sound (server authority resident, local-only readiness veto, abort-fenced late completions, disposal on every path) and fixed two defects with regression tests: camera churn beyond the retention radius (15 s linger) and a session-expiry reconnect that re-spawned the player at the District spawn (command-path expiry now keeps the departure grace like the broadcast loop). It also improved measured-byte accounting in the scheduler (test), notice naming and stale docs, and hardened the journey and matched harnesses (close-code records, planned-close socket rule, re-routing and wedge recovery, steering-tagged crossings, null-read guard). Evidence: ten journeys in all; on the merged head `8336cc52` runs 1–6 each completed 21–22 round trips with one harness-accounting or re-spawn failure, and run 7 with the server fix passed every check (22 trips, 22 evictions releasing all 8,239,722 bytes, after-twenty post-GC heap 1.008 / 1.028, 0 resource violations, 0 detours, 90 smooth crossings, both expiries resumed in place, hashes unchanged); the post-merge matched comparison passed on all four profiles (Market frame p95 equal to Gardens, CPU −1 %/+2 %/+11 %/+4 %, reconnect 1.09/0.89 s, +786 B transfer). Full detail: [the acceptance report](../acceptance/phase2-review-20261026.md).

Gaps (not blocking): (1) a bounded post-reconnect renderer step (+13 textures on one client in the loaded-host runs, +12 geometries after a session reconnect) outside the streamed Market; (2) run 7's loaded frame p95 reached 66.7 ms at one-minute load 10–17 (p50 33.4 ms), so the quiet-host runs 1–3 and the matched comparison are the frame-time evidence; (3) the harness keyboard walk remains load-sensitive above one-minute load ~15; (4) the server simulation was taken on a loaded host; (5) the source of the fixture identity's ~60-minute expiry was not traced (the dev-auth session TTL is seven days) and the client's reconnect gate shows the generic "Connection lost" text for a planned expiry; (6) no hosted, physical-device, production or human-accessibility evidence; (7) the core gate needed repeated attempts under other sessions' load (attempts 6, 7 and 8 passed in full).
