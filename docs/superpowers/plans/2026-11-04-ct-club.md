# CT Club: underground techno club room (position later)

- **Task:** `ct-club` (live register), owner `claude:river-oaks (ct-club)`.
- **Branch / worktree:** `feat/ribbonwing-viewer-upstream-20261104` in the primary checkout.
- **Request:** Val, 2026-11-04: "create a new thing, it is a special room with the
  design of an underground techno club" — entry, bar, dancefloor, couches/chairs,
  chai, big speaker and lights: four rare corner strobes, four RGB corner spots,
  a center disco ball with occasional RGB lasers, edge/back glow on the bar and
  edge glow on chairs and couches, edge-lit dancefloor tiles, 40 m × 6 m
  (20 × 3 bodies). "The precise position will be decided later."

## What was built

- `preview/src/ct-club-plan.js` — pure-JS plan (store-room frame shared
  with the server walker): zones, fixtures, collision obstacles, light positions,
  summary; `planCtClub({ east, north, yaw })` at any facade, default
  (0,0,0) is the pending position.
- `preview/src/ct-club-interior.js` — whole-room Three.js builder with
  instancing (two meshes = the whole LED dancefloor), emissive-only glows,
  `userData.tick(t)` animation (ball, RGB spots, lasers, rare strobes) and
  reduced-motion support; builds under node (no canvas).
- `preview/tests/ct-club.test.js` — 8 regressions.
- `preview/e2e/fixtures/ct-club.{html,js}` — design harness to inspect
  the room in the browser (tour, `sample()`).
- `docs/ct-club.md` — design notes ("chai" is read as both a chai urn
  on the bar and the edge-lit chairs; both built).
