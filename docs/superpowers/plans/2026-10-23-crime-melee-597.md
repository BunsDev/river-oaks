# #597 First criminal action: melee, rival health, wanted stars

## Objective and ownership

A fun, cheat-resistant first criminal action whose wanted consequences appear only where the
world rule (#596) permits. Task `crime-melee-597`, branch `feat/crime-melee-597-20261023`,
worktree `river-oaks-crime-597`, stacked on #596 (PR #603).

## Slices

1. **Server (this branch):** `server/crime.js` resolver, `attack` intent in `server/world.js`,
   snapshot `crime`, checkpoint v15, writer generation v17/v18, client snapshot validation, docs,
   unit, fuzz/forgery and transport tests.
2. **Client (same branch, second commit):** compact HUD (`crime-ui.js`): risk where you stand,
   server-confirmed hit, labelled rival health, wanted stars with a spoken label, down state and
   peaceful messaging. Also shove/stagger/down mapped to existing poses (`crime-poses.js`; new poses
   would invalidate the garment bakes, which hash `resident-gestures.js`), rivals drawn by the remote-player renderer,
   the T key, and the `crime-shared` two-browser journey.

## Decisions

- Eligible targets are authored rivals in the two danger zones; residents are protected; PvP also
  needs consent that nobody can give yet.
- Fixed 25 damage, 100 health, incapacitation at 0, full recovery 20 s after the last hit; stars
  0–5, −1 per 30 s of quiet; 0.9 s cooldown; 1.6 m reach, 0.6 m height, same room, sight line.
- Exactly once: per-attacker receipts for landed hits (60 s, 16, checkpointed). Redis commits
  already fence queue entries. No new event bus.
- Pre-v15 checkpoints accept only an untouched crime section.
