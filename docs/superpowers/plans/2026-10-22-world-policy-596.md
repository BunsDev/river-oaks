## Decisions

- Zones measured from `district.json`; danger zones are Westcreek Lane and the south service lane,
  safe zones the plaza and the Gardens; a test proves danger zones avoid every footprint and room.
- Half-open rectangles and windows; safe wins; indoors and upper levels are safe; attacks across
  zone boundaries refuse; malformed anything refuses.
- Residents do not follow the shared clock (hour stays 15); tested.
- Emergency off bypasses revision, budget and cooldown; updates need the current revision.
- Pre-v14 checkpoints may carry only untouched (revision 0) rules, like an empty campus section.
- Protocol unchanged: the snapshot field is optional and validated when present.
