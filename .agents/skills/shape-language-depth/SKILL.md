# Shape Language & Depth

Read the [shared working contract](../../../docs/level-design-skills.md#shared-working-contract)
and [skill safety and validation contract](../../../docs/level-design/skill-suite-contract.md).

## Inputs and inspection

Require the approved reference intent, footprint/entrance constraints, current
blockout, runtime/controller profile and matched stations. Use
**Reference-to-Scene Comparison** first; label unmatched photographs as qualitative.
Inspect silhouette, primary/secondary masses, facade rhythm and the depth of
entrances, awnings, bays and recesses. Tie every proposed form to shelter,
threshold, structure, activity or recognition. Preserve intentional stylization,
accessible cues and mapped anchors; visual complexity alone is not improvement.

Measure in meters with the current scale buddy and grid. At player height, check
whether the entrance reads before surface detail is added. Separate work/queue
space from through travel. Record observed geometry separately from hypotheses
about why it feels flat.

## Modification

When alternatives are requested, preserve the baseline and build two small,
recoverable variants of one representative frontage: for example a recessed entry
and a projecting canopy. Keep camera/settings fixed. State each variant's spatial
purpose, dimensions and tradeoff; do not extrude every panel or alter the whole
street. Use **Blockout Builder**, **Map Scale & Blockout** and **Room Logic** for
geometry, scale and activity constraints. Approval of one variant precedes broad
application or final art.

## Acceptance and output

Return baseline/A/B matched views, dimension and purpose tables, route results,
remaining hypotheses and a recommendation. Pass only when the intended entrance
and hierarchy are legible in reviewed player-height views and affected traversal
passes. Missing matched views means visual comparison is incomplete; blocked
travel fails even if a variant looks better. Restore the baseline variant and
rerun its route if the change regresses clearance. Hand off to **Blockout Review**.

## Examples and validation

Use the [shape-language-depth fixture](../../../docs/level-design/skill-suite-fixtures.md#shape-language-depth)
for a successful-use example and a failure case. Execute its inspection scenario
before claiming skill validation; follow the fixture's proof limits.
